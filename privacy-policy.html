Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Midnight Uploads Studio is a locally operated desktop content-production tool.

Information we process

  • The tool processes themes, scripts, captions, locally selected video and music files, and generated video files on the user’s computer.
  • When the user connects YouTube or TikTok, those services provide authorization tokens. Tokens and API credentials are stored locally on the user’s computer and are used only to perform actions the user requests.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

The tool may send content and account-authorized requests to OpenAI, YouTube/Google, TikTok, and text-to-speech providers selected by the user. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and control

Local drafts, media, settings, and authorization tokens remain on the user’s computer until the user deletes them. Connected-platform access can be revoked through the relevant Google or TikTok account settings.
